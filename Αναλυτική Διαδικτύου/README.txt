Main Idea:

HumaNature is an app that helps bring humans closer to nature by providing carefully curated outdoor activities based on the user's preferences and interests. The user fills out a brief questionnaire while also being able to describe their ideal adventure in further detail in the end. Based on the response of the user, the app recommends trips that fit the criteria of the questionnaire.
